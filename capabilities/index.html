How a product is made

From the first drawing to the finished part - every stage happens under one roof.

  1. Drawing & costing — Customer sends a drawing, a sample or an idea. We study the part, suggest changes where needed and give the costing.
  2. 3D design & mould design — The product is modelled in 3D, then the mould is designed around it - cavity layout, cooling, ejection and gating.
  3. 3D printed prototype — An FDM printed sample is made so the customer can hold the part and approve the shape before the mould is cut.
  4. Mould manufacturing — Core and cavity are machined on the VMC, detailed on the EDM, then fitted and polished by hand.
  5. Mould trial — The mould is loaded on the injection moulding machine and trialled. Shot weight, cycle and cooling are set until the part comes out right.
  6. Production — Once approved, the mould runs in production. Parts are checked during the run, not only at the end.
  7. Final product — Finished parts are inspected, counted, packed and dispatched.

Mould steels and systems
